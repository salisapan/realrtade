# v2.3: the small on-device model learns what the rules miss

Glance closes open loops. Gmail is where it starts today.

Lab only. v2.3 is a shadow candidate: nothing in the extension loads it, and nothing it decides is shown to anyone. v2 stays the default.

## What was wrong with the question

The v2 reference label is the rules engine itself (engine 0.9.35 on the clean render, plus product overrides). A model trained on it can at best copy the engine. "v2 misses about half the closes" is v2 **alone**, measured against the engine's own labels. In the product the engine runs first, so on those labels v2 adds almost nothing. The number that matters is what the model adds where the engine is silent.

The rows the engine is silent on although they are ask-shaped and addressed to the person (`reference.unsure`, the engine-recall gap) were left out of v2's training. Many are real asks: "Can I get the budget spreadsheet from you next Tuesday?", «אפשר להעלות את תעודת ההתאגדות למערכת עד יום חמישי?», «נתראה מחר ב-14:30 במשרד».

## What v2.3 changes

`train/gap-labels-v23.cjs` labels a gap row from its template's intent, only where the product rules would accept the label: inbound, the person on To and addressed directly (group greetings and Cc stay unsure: a profile feature), a date the device reads (a meeting also needs a time), and a scenario with one clear label. Undated asks stay unsure: that is the owner's open decision. The labels are template intent, **not owner-verified**.

Same features, same trainer (`train/train-v2.py --feat features-v23`), same tau rule (val wrong Do It ≤ 0.29%).

## Numbers (v2 test split, 6,982 rows; both models trained on the same rebuilt dataset)

The dataset was rebuilt with `run-cpu-checks.sh` step 4. Its sha256 does not match `dataset/v2/all.jsonl.sha256` (the same mismatch the AI-engine routine reported), so both models were trained on the rebuilt set and compared to each other, not to the historical report.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2, model alone | 5 / 4,938 | 661 / 1,252 | 37 | 46 / 315 (HE 39/263, EN 7/52) |
| v2.3, model alone | 4 / 4,938 | 592 / 1,252 | 60 | 96 / 315 (HE 88/263, EN 8/52) |

| **engine + veto, then v2.3** | **9 / 4,938** | **32 / 1,252** | **14** | **96 / 315** |

In the product order, v2.3 catches twice as many of the engine's blind spots (96 against 46) with one fewer wrong Do It. 14 gap rows get a Do It of the wrong kind (9 with v2). English gap recall stays low (8/52): most gap rows are Hebrew. Strict wrong Do It (unsure rows counted as silent): 88 with v2, 51 with v2.3.

Runtime parity: the JS int8 runtime reproduces 6,974 / 6,982 Python decisions with v2.3 (6,977 with v2): `node runtime/parity-check-tag.cjs v23`.

## What it does not prove

The gap labels come from the template's intent, so this measures generalisation to unseen templates of the same generator, not real mail. Promotion over v2 goes through `glance-ai/oss/finetune/promote_gate.cjs` and needs 200 owner-verified labels.

## CORRECTION 2026-11-11: label ids are positional; the numbers since 2026-11-08 are NOT valid

Found while checking a baseline: `agreement-gold.jsonl`, the batch label files and the overlay are keyed by dataset row id (`v2syn-22856`), and those ids are positional. Any generator change (a new template) renumbers rows and moves texts. After the 2026-11-08 rebuild only 172 of the 500 labelled texts still exist in the dataset (165 under the same id, 7 under another); the other 301 are gone (27 ambiguous). Every score since then that joined labels to `out-v2/test.jsonl` by id (the runs of 2026-11-08, 2026-11-11 including "v2.6 passes the gate", C-3 on `v2syn-22856`, v2.7, v2.8, the frame features, the first frontier baseline) scored many labels against the wrong text. **Treat the label-set results as withdrawn** (the test-split numbers against the dataset's own reference labels, taken on one rebuilt dataset and compared model against model, do not use the label files and stand, but they only measure agreement with the engine-derived reference). Runs up to 2026-10-26 used the pinned dataset and are not affected. Fix, in this commit: the label set is frozen as text (`labeling/frozen-eval.jsonl`, 491 rows, built by `model/train/export-frozen.cjs` from the batch files, which carry text with labels), `train-v2.py --predict-set` predicts exactly those rows, and `scoreboard/run.cjs` scores them without reading the dataset ids. Re-measured results follow in the next section.

Full frozen set, agreement labels n = 485 (264 silent, 221 act), product order, engine hard quiets as a veto (`--hard-quiets A`): engine alone 6.4% wrong Do It / 50.7% missed; engine + v2 6.4% / 46.2%; engine + v2.3 6.4% / 37.1%; engine + v2.6 6.4% / 35.3%; **engine + hard quiets, then zero-shot Claude 7.2% (19 / 264) / 2.3% (5 / 221)**; zero-shot Claude alone 9.8% (26 / 264) / 4.1% (9 / 221). Wrong actions: engine 12, v2 15, v2.3 and v2.6 17, Claude after the engine 31, Claude alone 33.

Reading: (1) the engine floor plus a strong reader keeps a wrong Do It rate close to the engine's own (7.2% against 6.4%) and takes recall from 46% to 98%; without the floor the same reader acts wrongly 9.8%. (2) The small on-device models close part of the recall gap (46% to 35% missed) at no wrong Do It, the cloud-class reader closes almost all of it. (3) The wrong-action column is the open problem: the reader's 31 are mostly the C-7 kind question (task or reply draft) and some label questions. (4) The labels are Claude's under the same rules the baseline was given: the baseline is as favoured as it can be, so a result in its favour proves little and the numbers say nothing about being better than a frontier model. v2 stays the default; nothing is wired.

### E5 (plan 2): a boosted second level over the linear gate, no gain (2026-11-11)

`train/train-stack.py`: level 1 is the v2 logistic gate and chooser (grouped OOF); level 2 is a HistGradientBoosting gate over the level-1 score plus the engine's own decision and vetoes, language, addressee voc and role, attachments, length and 12 speech-act frames (all available on the device); tau chosen on level-2 OOF at the same wrong-Do-It target. Frozen set, agreement n = 565, product order with the engine hard quiets as a veto:

| model | wrong Do It | missed | wrong action |
|---|---|---|---|
| engine + v2 | 6.7% | 43.6% | 15 |
| engine + v2.9 | 6.4% | 37.6% | 17 |
| engine + stack | 7.4% | 42.9% | 14 |

Hold-out (007 + 008): stack 7.4% and 45.6% against v2.9's 6.2% and 41.1%. **The pre-registered threshold (at least 4 points fewer misses at equal wrong Do It, on dev and hold-out) is not met; the second level is no better than v2 and worse than v2.9; model alone it is worse (15.1% wrong Do It).** Reading, with the frame-feature result: a more expressive learner over the same template-generated rows does not help. The limit is the training signal (labels derived from the engine, synthetic phrasing), not the learner's capacity. The next experiment therefore changes the signal: teacher labels (the reader's, under the labelling rules) on a sample of training rows.

### E8-lite (plan 2): teacher labels on 320 training rows, no gain at this scale (2026-11-11)

Two subagents (a strong reader under `labeling/pass-rules.md`, one pass) labelled 320 stratified TRAINING rows (`labeling/teacher-sample.jsonl`, `labeling/teacher-labels.jsonl`; no frozen, hold-out or sealed text). The teacher answered SILENT 159, CONTEXT 69, task 33, draft 29, calendar 16, file_save 13, UNSURE 1. **Against the engine-derived reference:** of 249 rows the reference calls silent, the teacher would offer 36 (14%: calendar 12, file save 9, task 8, draft 7); of the act rows it changes the step on 10 (draft to task 4, calendar to draft 2, task to silent 5). So about one reference silence in seven is a close the reader sees. v3.0 (`train/gap-labels-v30.cjs`) trains on v2.9's labels with those 250 usable teacher rows replacing the reference and repeated 4 times:

| model | wrong Do It | missed | wrong action |
|---|---|---|---|
| engine + v2.9 | 6.4% | 37.6% | 17 |
| engine + v3.0 | 6.4% | 36.8% | 16 |

Hold-out (007 + 008): v3.0 6.2% and 42.2% against v2.9's 6.2% and 41.1%. **No effect at this scale** (320 rows are about 1% of the training data; the difference is inside the noise, see `scoreboard/power.cjs`). Distillation only matters with the teacher's labels at a scale the CPU lab cannot buy through subagents without the account's usage limit: tens of thousands of rows need a reader service, not chat passes.

**Summary of the "stronger small model" experiments (owner request 2026-11-11):** frame features (v3a), a boosted second level (E5), more engine-gap labels (v2.7, v2.8), the Hebrew and kind-of-action labels (v2.9) and 320 teacher labels (v3.0) all land at about 36 to 38% missed at 6.4% wrong Do It on the frozen set. The small model has plateaued; the remaining recall is the reader's job (2.3% missed under the same floor), which is Phase 4 and needs a reader service or a GPU host.


### v3.2 and v3.3 (plan 2, Phase D on CPU, 2026-11-12): consistent teacher supervision breaks the plateau

No API call and no spend: the 10,000 teacher labels of the lab run (`labeling/teacher/teacher-labels-final.jsonl`, Haiku 5.5 with escalation to Sonnet 5.5; ids checked against the current dataset text: 10,000 of 10,000 match). Frozen and validation numbers below are Claude's two-pass agreement labels, NOT owner-verified; product order, engine hard quiets veto the model (`--hard-quiets A`). Lab only: nothing wired, v2 stays the default.

- **v3.1 reproduced locally** (`train/gap-labels-v31.cjs`, `TEACHER_LABELS=teacher/teacher-labels-final.jsonl K=1`): 36.8% missed on the frozen set and 38.9% on validation, the same as the lab session's report. The teacher disagreed with the engine-derived reference on about 45% of its rows, so v3.1 learned two conventions at once.
- **v3.2, teacher-only supervision** (`train/gap-labels-v32.cjs`): train on the 7,977 teacher rows only, every reference-labelled row masked. The teacher labelled training rows only, so the split that picks C is 15% of the teacher threads (by thread id, never split) and the original val rows are left out. Frozen: 6.7% wrong Do It (20/299), **22.6% missed** (60/266), 30 wrong actions. Validation: 7.4% and **24.4% missed**. Held by the gate on wrong actions (15 -> 30) and one more Hebrew wrong Do It.
- **v3.2r, residual** (`MODE=residual`, only rows where the rules engine is silent and not hard-quiet): frozen 6.4% and 28.2%, 28 wrong actions; worse recall than v3.2, kept as a record.
- **Diagnosis** (`scoreboard/confusion.cjs`, product-order confusion; declared: read on the frozen set and on the validation batch): the extra wrong actions were the model's draft -> task (16 of 18). The teacher had broken a written rule: `labeling/pass-rules.md` makes an ask to pay the SENDER a draft ("a payment BACK"), and Sonnet labelled the generator's `payment` family (pay us) a task in 302 of 367 reference-draft rows, plus 78 reference-silent rows.
- **v3.3** (`train/gap-labels-v33.cjs`): v3.2 with one audit rule on the teacher's labels: a teacher `task` on a `payment` row becomes `follow-up-ask|draft` (382 rows); paying a third party stays a task. Plus a step-confidence floor (`train-v2.py --action-target 0.05`, off by default): the model stays silent when the chooser is unsure of the step; kappa = 0.62 is chosen on grouped OOF over training rows (wrong action 5% of the shown asks), not on any label set.

**Stop rule (4 points on the validation batch at the same wrong-Do-It rate): met.** v3.3 misses 8.9 points fewer than v3.1 on validation at the same 5/81 wrong Do Its (17.8 points fewer than v2 at one fewer).

**Promotion gate (`promote_gate.cjs` decide against v2):** validation: no more wrong Do Its, no more wrong actions (10 = 10), fewer misses in both languages, verdict "better on spec labels" (171 agreement rows, under the 200 the gate requires). Whole frozen set: fewer wrong Do Its (19 vs 20) and far fewer misses, **HOLD on wrong actions (15 -> 17)**: the model makes 5 (3 draft -> task, 2 task -> draft), v2's model 3. Not tuned further on the frozen set; the next lever is more teacher rows with the payment rule stated in the prompt (below).

**What it means:** the plateau at 36 to 38% was the supervision, not the learner: the same linear gate and chooser, trained on one consistent convention, close about 18 points more of the frozen set's asks at no extra wrong Do It. **What it does not prove:** the labels are Claude's (teacher and agreement passes share a family and its blind spots), the mails are generated, and the frozen and validation sets have been read many times. No "better than a frontier model" claim; the frontier reader under the same floor still misses far less (2.3% frozen, 2.6% sealed).
