# v2.3: the small on-device model learns what the rules miss

Glance closes open loops. Gmail is where it starts today.

Lab only. v2.3 is a shadow candidate: nothing in the extension loads it, and nothing it decides is shown to anyone. v2 stays the default.

## What was wrong with the question

The v2 reference label is the rules engine itself (engine 0.9.35 on the clean render, plus product overrides). A model trained on it can at best copy the engine. "v2 misses about half the closes" is v2 **alone**, measured against the engine's own labels. In the product the engine runs first, so on those labels v2 adds almost nothing. The number that matters is what the model adds where the engine is silent.

The rows the engine is silent on although they are ask-shaped and addressed to the person (`reference.unsure`, the engine-recall gap) were left out of v2's training. Many are real asks: "Can I get the budget spreadsheet from you next Tuesday?", «אפשר להעלות את תעודת ההתאגדות למערכת עד יום חמישי?», «נתראה מחר ב-14:30 במשרד».

## What v2.3 changes

`train/gap-labels-v23.cjs` labels a gap row from its template's intent, only where the product rules would accept the label: inbound, the person on To and addressed directly (group greetings and Cc stay unsure: a profile feature), a date the device reads (a meeting also needs a time), and a scenario with one clear label. Undated asks stay unsure: that is the owner's open decision. The labels are template intent, **not owner-verified**.

Same features, same trainer (`train/train-v2.py --feat features-v23`), same tau rule (val wrong Do It ≤ 0.29%).

## Numbers (v2 test split, 6,982 rows; both models trained on the same rebuilt dataset)

The dataset was rebuilt with `run-cpu-checks.sh` step 4. Its sha256 does not match `dataset/v2/all.jsonl.sha256` (the same mismatch the AI-engine routine reported), so both models were trained on the rebuilt set and compared to each other, not to the historical report.

| system | wrong Do It (reference silent) | missed (reference show) | wrong action | engine blind spots caught (gap rows) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2, model alone | 5 / 4,938 | 661 / 1,252 | 37 | 46 / 315 (HE 39/263, EN 7/52) |
| v2.3, model alone | 4 / 4,938 | 592 / 1,252 | 60 | 96 / 315 (HE 88/263, EN 8/52) |
| engine + veto, then v2 | 10 / 4,938 | 32 / 1,252 | 14 | 46 / 315 |
| **engine + veto, then v2.3** | **9 / 4,938** | **32 / 1,252** | **14** | **96 / 315** |

In the product order, v2.3 catches twice as many of the engine's blind spots (96 against 46) with one fewer wrong Do It. 14 gap rows get a Do It of the wrong kind (9 with v2). English gap recall stays low (8/52): most gap rows are Hebrew. Strict wrong Do It (unsure rows counted as silent): 88 with v2, 51 with v2.3.

Runtime parity: the JS int8 runtime reproduces 6,974 / 6,982 Python decisions with v2.3 (6,977 with v2): `node runtime/parity-check-tag.cjs v23`.

## Reproduce (CPU, about 5 minutes)

```sh
# dataset as in run-cpu-checks.sh step 4 (GLANCE_TIP, GLANCE_V2_ENGINE=0.9.35, GLANCE_NOW, GLANCE_ENGINE_ROOTS)
node model/dataset/build-dataset.cjs && node model/dataset/v2/build-dataset-v2.cjs
cd model && node train/export-features-v2.cjs
GLANCE_NOW=2026-10-07T12:00:00+03:00 node train/gap-labels-v23.cjs
.venv/bin/python train/train-v2.py --tag base --out v23/base --no-export
.venv/bin/python train/train-v2.py --feat features-v23 --tag v23 --out v23/export
.venv/bin/python train/compare-v23.py v23/base/base.test-preds.jsonl
```

## What it does not prove

The gap labels come from the template's intent, so this measures generalisation to unseen templates of the same generator, not real mail. Promotion over v2 goes through `glance-ai/oss/finetune/promote_gate.cjs` and needs 200 owner-verified labels.

## Run 2026-10-12 (model trainer): v2.3 stays the best so far

The dataset was rebuilt the same way and v2.3 reproduced to the byte (`artifacts/v23.test-preds.jsonl`). Two v2.4 candidates were trained (`train/gap-labels-v24.cjs`; compare with `train/compare-v2n.py`):
- v2.4 counts the deadlines "today", "by end of week", «היום» and «סוף השבוע» as dated (the device's parser does not read them), and labels dated notes to self as tasks;
- the same with a tau target of 0.20% instead of 0.29%.

Neither is promoted, because neither passes on every label set:
- v2.4 has 11 wrong Do Its on the test split against 9;
- the 0.20% variant has as many wrong Do Its but misses more on the Claude batches and catches fewer blind spots (84/315 against 96).

The numbers and the gate verdicts are in `glance-ai/labeling/BATCH-004.md`. On the new hold-out (batch-004, 93 scored), the product order with v2.3 has 7/53 wrong Do Its, all from the engine. It misses 24/40: Hebrew 8/19, English 16/21.

## Run 2026-10-19: the dataset now matches its pinned hash; v2.3 stays the best so far

Two lab bugs made every earlier number here slightly off:
- the lab teachers ran the engine on the real clock, not `GLANCE_NOW`;
- `engine.cjs` called today's engine without the recipients and the person's names.

Both are fixed in `model/teacher/`. With the clock frozen, `dataset/out-v2/all.jsonl` matches `dataset/v2/all.jsonl.sha256` exactly. The sha256 mismatch noted above was the real clock.

Re-measured on the pinned dataset, product order, step level (`train/compare-v2n.py`):

| model | wrong Do It | missed | engine blind spots caught |
|---|---|---|---|
| v2 | 9 / 4,935 | 31 / 1,255 | 47 / 367 |
| **v2.3** | **10 / 4,935** | **26 / 1,255** | **138 / 367 (EN 13/52)** |

Two v2.5 candidates (`train/gap-labels-v25.cjs`) were held:
- v2.5a passes the routine's rule, but the gate holds it on one more miss;
- v2.5b passes the gate, but fails the routine's rule on 2 more wrong Do Its: "asap" read as a deadline.

On the 354 two-pass agreement labels, the gate also holds v2.3 against v2 (26 against 25 wrong Do Its), so v2 stays the default. Details: `glance-ai/labeling/BATCH-005.md`.
