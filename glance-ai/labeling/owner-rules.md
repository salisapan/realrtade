# Owner labelling rules

Glance closes open loops. Gmail is where it starts today.

Product rules the owner decided for labels. A rule here overrides an older reading in `lib.cjs` or a batch note. Each rule is dated.

## 3b. A confirmed amount with no ask is silence (owner «מאשר», 2026-10-14)

A mail that confirms an amount or an approved budget and asks the owner for nothing is **SILENT**: «אישרנו 4,200», "the budget was approved", "Confirming the fee is about $4,200". It overrides the older `confirmed-amount|task` reading in `lib.cjs` for labels. Seven of the 13 two-pass disagreements in batch-003 were this case (`BATCH-003.md`). The engine still offers that task today; aligning it is a separate product change behind its own switch.

Source: `docs/glance-ai/proposals/2026-10-12-owner-decisions.md` (3b), `docs/vault/decisions.md` 2026-10-14.
