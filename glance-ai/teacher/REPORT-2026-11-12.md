## Result: the stop rule fired

Validation batch (007 + 008, two-pass agreement, n = 171; 81 reference-show rows for wrong Do It, 90 for missed), product order with the engine hard quiets:

| system | wrong Do It | missed |
|---|---|---|
| engine + v2 (default) | 7.4% (6/81) | 47.8% (43/90) |
| engine + v2.9 (best prior) | 6.2% (5/81) | 41.1% (37/90) |
| engine + **v3.1** (10k teacher labels) | 6.2% (5/81) | **38.9% (35/90)** |

v3.1 cut missed closes by 2.2 points against v2.9 (the rule needs at least 4 at the same or lower wrong-Do-It rate) and by 8.9 against v2. Against the best prior the gain is under the bar, and 2 rows out of 90 is inside noise (Wilson interval 29.5% to 49.2%). **Stop rule fired: no further spend, no request for more money.** The promotion gate also says HOLD for v3.1 (wrong actions 10 to 11 on the validation batch, 15 to 20 on the frozen set).

Frozen set (565 rows, valid scoreboard): engine + v3.1 6.4% wrong Do It / 36.8% missed (98/266), against engine + v2 6.7% / 43.6%, engine + v2.9 6.4% / 37.6%. Wrong actions rose (15 for v2, 17 for v2.9, 20 for v3.1). Full tables: `scoreboard-frozen-v31.md`, `scoreboard-validation-v31.md`.

Sealed batch-009 opened once (logged in `labeling/sealed/README.md`, tables in `labeling/sealed/opening-2026-11-11.md`; the batch is now a hold-out). n = 77 agreement rows: frontier reader (Opus 5.5) missed 2.6% (1/38) with 3/39 wrong Do Its; engine+v2 31.6% / 5.1%; engine+v2.9 23.7% / 5.1%. Teacher vs generator reference: the generator calls about a thousand rows SILENT that the teacher calls task, draft or calendar.
