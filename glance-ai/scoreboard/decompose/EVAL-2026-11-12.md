# Honest evaluation before any spend: Decompose v1 and the in-house model, 2026-11-12

Glance closes open loops. Gmail is where it starts today.

Lab only. Nothing is wired, merged to `main` or deployed; `aiDecomposeV1` is off and no host calls it; v2 stays the default. No API call was made for this evaluation ($0; ledger total stays $2.591). Labels are Claude's and NOT owner-verified everywhere below. The sealed set was not opened.

## 0. What a clause and a whole mail contain

The whole-mail judge gets: the mail's new text, the subject, the sender, To and Cc, the person's own addresses and names, the direction, the attachment count and the surface. No thread history. A clause gets the same host context with only the text changed: the greeting plus that one clause (the connector word "Also," / «בנוסף,» removed). So a clause cannot see what another clause said; that is why the floor below exists.

## 1. Found during this evaluation: a hole, now closed

The natural set (section 3) caught Decompose v1 as pushed in `d166efd5` offering a reply draft on **"Could you send the invoice? Please don't."** The whole mail was a close-family quiet, and v1 ran the clause pass on every family quiet. Narrowed, each with a corpus check (`test/intent-decompose-corpus.cjs`, 45 checks):

1. a family quiet gets a clause pass only for the two-clocks rule (two meetings; family H `ambiguousClocks`), and only when the host hands in both the family check and the phishing check;
2. a clause that takes something back anywhere in the mail (don't, no need, never mind, instead, ignore, cancel, "or could you"; «אל ת», «לא צריך», «במקום», «תתעלם») stops the clause pass for the whole mail;
3. an intention beyond the first is offered only when its own clause carries its own day or clock (a context sentence such as "We need your sign-off." was becoming a second card).

Every number below is after the narrowing.

## 3. The real multi-intent set: it does not exist in the lab

Searched every lab pool for mails WRITTEN with two or more ask-shaped sentences (4,315 hand-written and model-written mails outside the template generator, 3,022 unique texts; the about 28,500 template rows carry one intention per mail by construction). Result: **33 mails**, saved as `labeling/multi-clause-natural-v0.jsonl` (eval only, never a training row; gold written by hand before the engine ran on them; 1 pass, 5 left undecided). `real-mail/private/` holds 0 rows; the only real text in the lab is 48 masked sentences.

Of the 28 decided mails, **27 carry one closable intent or none** (the second sentence corrects, cancels, hedges or explains the first) and **exactly 1 carries two kinds of close** ("Can we meet this Thursday, October 8, at 10:00...? Also, please send me the updated pricing sheet by Wednesday.").

| system (natural set, n = 28, 27 gold intents) | every intent right, nothing extra | intents missed | offered where gold is silence |
|---|---|---|---|
| engine, whole mail | 9/28 | 19/27 | 0/2 |
| decomposed engine (before the narrowing) | 11/28 | 14/27 | **1/2** |
| decomposed engine (now) | 10/28 | 18/27 | 0/2 |

The one two-kind mail: the whole-mail engine found the meeting only; the decomposed engine found both. **n = 1 is an anecdote, not a rate. All-intents-found on real multi-intent mail cannot be measured with the lab's data, so the stop rule's condition (a clear margin on the real set, not only on stitched mail) is NOT met today.** The set is useful for the opposite risk (a clause that takes an ask back), which is how the hole was found.

Why 18 intents are still missed there: 11 are file asks the file chain owns (`file`, `file-chain-not-run`: the lab has no Drive, so it cannot tell whether the product would offer), 3 are hedge quiets ("Please don't send the invoice. Please send the contract."), 2 are family quiets on alternatives, 2 are a task where the label says a reply ("approve ... by October 14").

## 4. What the 89.3% / 98% figures were, and what they are now

They were measured on **stitched mail**: two training mails joined by "Also," / «בנוסף,», restricted to parts the engine already reads right alone. They test whether the split loses an intention that the engine can read. **They do not test recognition on a mail a person wrote with two intentions, and they are not a Glance quality number.**

After the narrowing the same stitched reading is lower, because an undated second ask is now left to the first card:

With all parts (583 stitched mails, not filtered): 25.2% to 43.2%. Safety on 7,977 teacher-labelled training mails (4,542 silent): the clause pass creates **0** new offers on a silent mail and finds 5 more right closes.
