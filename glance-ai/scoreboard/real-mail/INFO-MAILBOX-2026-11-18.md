# The connected Flow info@ mailbox, read on 2026-11-18 (counts only)

Owner, 2026-11-18: «תעבור על כל המיילים שלי שאתה מחובר אליהם ותאמן את המודל על גביהם». Product definition (vision-locked): Glance closes open loops; Gmail is where it starts today. Current implementation status: lab measurement only; nothing here turns a switch on or changes a scope.

**What the mailbox is.** The Gmail connector reads one account (the Flow info@ business mailbox, about 201 threads in every view the connector gives). It is a young mailbox: about 60% forwarded service mail (security alerts, product marketing, sign-in codes), about 50 outbound cold outreach sends of one template, about 20 test mails written for Glance's own live Gate (`[G-...]` subjects), a few auto-replies and bounces. **It holds almost no real person-to-person mail**, so it is a small pool for finding gaps, not a training corpus.

**Where the text lives.** Masked rows are in `glance-ai/scoreboard/real-mail/private/info-mailbox-2026-11-18.jsonl` (26 rows), gitignored, never committed, never pasted. It is a pool for looking at gaps and for counts. It is not the frozen real-mail set (`REAL-MAILBOX-2026-11-12.md`), which stays untouched and is never trained on. No weights were trained on it: a model trained on private mail would carry that mail, and the repository must not.

**What it found (counts, as shipped with waves 1 and 2).** Of 16 test-gate mails and 10 others: 1 wrong offer on a real shape and 0 on the rest. The wrong offer: a Hebrew out-of-office reply («לא במשרד ולא זמינה, יש ליצור קשר עם המשרד בטלפון ... מיום חזרתי לפעילות») was offered as a reply draft, because its own request sentence read as a request. Fixed in `core/intent.js` (`HE_AUTO_REPLY`, and the Hebrew out-of-office phrase is cut before a clause is read) and pinned in `test/direct-ask-corpus.cjs`. The same real shape exposed a second gap: «אני לא במשרד ביום חמישי, אפשר שיחת וידאו ביום שלישי ב-10:00?» was vetoed as an auto-reply; «שיחת וידאו» was not an event word. Fixed the same way.

**Also corrected here.** The wave-2 note of 2026-11-16 listed a Sheet row among the closes that are on. It is not: `core/sheet-row.js` is a lab reader that no `src/` file calls, and it needs a Sheets scope the manifest does not ask. Wave 2 is WORKSPACE_FILE (empty Doc, Sheet or Slides file), MEET_LINK, TEAMS_MEETING and ONEDRIVE_NEW_FILE. The measurement no longer counts the Sheet row.

It is a different account from the one approved before, and its content is other people's confidential matters, so nothing from it was ingested or stored; only the three newest subjects of automated state-registry receipts were seen to confirm the connection.
