Where Flow runs: three setups.

Regulated teams ask two questions: where the data goes, and who can compel access to it. Flow answers with three setups, and the office chooses the one its policy calls for. In all three, Flow prepares the work and a person approves it before anything is written.

The three setups

1. Flow's dedicated secure cloud

Flow runs in its own dedicated secure cloud, and masks all sensitive data before any processing. Names, identifiers and other sensitive fields are replaced before a model sees the material. This is the lightest setup to start with.

2. Flow-Edge

For computers with suitable hardware. Processing happens on the employee's own computer: data is stored only there and never leaves it.

3. Flow-Onprem

A dedicated server inside the office's internal network. Data never goes to an external cloud.

Residency is not sovereignty

Residency is where the bytes sit. Sovereignty is who can demand them. A region pin answers the first question. It does not, by itself, answer a compel, a subpoena, or a vendor that can read the payload. Name which question the policy is actually asking before you pick a setup.

How to choose

  • Masked payloads, a contract that limits use, and a human gate before any write can be enough for some teams: that is the dedicated secure cloud.
  • When policy says client material stays on the employee's machine: Flow-Edge, on computers with suitable hardware.
  • When policy says it stays inside the office network, on a server the office controls: Flow-Onprem.

The policy decides. A slogan about "private AI" does not. Bring the policy that governs the material, the systems a write would touch, and who must approve it, and the setup follows from them.

What stays the same in all three

  • Flow prepares; a person approves. Flow does not auto-send or auto-write.
  • Execution is a defined step, not a series of guessed clicks. What approval has to show.
  • Security controls such as SSO and the audit log scope are agreed with your security team in the engagement. The security page describes the architecture.

Common questions

Does Flow send documents to a public model by default?

No. In the dedicated secure cloud, sensitive data is masked before any processing. With Flow-Edge it stays on the employee's computer. With Flow-Onprem it stays on a server in your network.

Can we change setups later?

Setup is chosen with the office in the engagement. Talk to us about moving between them. This page is product framing, not legal advice.
