What just happened

No model read your email. A scorer did — and it can show its work.

Every verdict above came from named signals with fixed weights, added up and compared against one threshold. That's why each card can tell you why, line by line, instead of asking you to trust a black box.

01 · Signals, not keywords

A currency figure is worth 34. An outright authorisation, 42. A dated obligation, 26. Mailing-list boilerplate subtracts 45. The sum is the score you saw.

02 · One moving threshold

A fresh install speaks at 50. In the extension that number moves as you click and dismiss — so it gets quieter or sharper for you, without a settings screen.

03 · On your device

The scorer is plain pattern work — no network call, no model. That's why this page could run it with nothing to install, and why the extension can promise the same thing inside Gmail.
