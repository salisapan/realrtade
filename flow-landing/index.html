HOW IT WORKS

One loop, from the moment to the done.

Flow watches for the moment something happens, prepares what comes next, and finishes it when you say so.

It notices what happened

A deal closes, a contract is signed, a case is approved. Flow sees the moment and works out what needs to happen next — no rules to write.

You approve with one click

A single Do It button appears at the right moment, with the update already prepared across your files and stack. High-risk steps always wait for your approval.

It learns how your experts decide

Flow picks up how your senior people handle real cases and replicates that judgment to the rest of the team.

Flow is pre-certification. We will tell you in writing exactly where each framework stands, rather than have you infer it from a badge. Read the security architecture →
