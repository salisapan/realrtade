Setting up your Pro key…

This takes a few seconds. Your key will appear here and is also emailed to you.

Activate it

  1. In Chrome, open the Glance panel (the Glance icon in your toolbar).
  2. Under Glance Pro, choose I have a key, paste the key and press Activate.
  3. Open an email in Gmail. Draft-It and attachment summaries are now on.

Don’t have Glance yet? Get the extension — it’s free. To cancel or change your plan, open the Glance panel and choose Manage billing, or reply to your key email.
