Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Flow ("Flow", "we", "us") collects when you visit theflow-ai.com, join our early-access waitlist, or install the Flow Trial Chrome extension — how it's used, and the choices you have. Flow is currently in pre-release, early-access status; this policy will be expanded as the product moves toward general availability.

Two things are covered here, and they are separate products. Flow Trial is the free Chrome extension described in Section 5 — it is intended for general business use, not for regulated or otherwise sensitive material, and it carries none of the contractual or compliance guarantees described on our Security page. Flow (the core product) is deployed for an organization under its own agreement, in a masked cloud tenant or on the customer's own infrastructure, and its data handling is governed by that agreement rather than by this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, and a small amount of information automatically.

  • Waitlist information. When you join the waitlist we collect your work email address, and — once you complete signup — your company name, role, and company website.
  • Communications. If you email us, we keep that correspondence to respond to you and keep a record of the interaction.
  • Automatic / analytics information. Like most sites, we use an analytics tool (Google Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic — pages viewed, general location by country/region, device and browser type, and referral source. This is not tied to your name unless you've also given us your email separately.
  • Local preferences. We store a light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device and is not sent to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage the waitlist and grant early access when it opens.
  • To send you the Hybrid Automation Playbook and related onboarding material you requested, and to follow up about a discovery call.
  • To understand how the site and product content are used, so we can improve them.
  • To detect and prevent abuse (for example, automated/bot signups).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your waitlist submission to train third-party AI models.

3. Email Confirmation

To keep our mailing list accurate and to prevent someone from signing up an email address that isn't theirs, we use a confirmation step: after you submit the waitlist form, we send a confirmation link to the email address provided. The Playbook and further communications are sent only after that link is clicked.

4. Who We Share Information With

We share information only with the service providers that operate the waitlist and site on our behalf, under their own data-processing terms:

  • Supabase — stores waitlist submissions (email, company, role, website) and enterprise enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • Resend — delivers transactional email (confirmation link, Playbook, discovery-call follow-up).
  • Netlify — hosts the site and runs the serverless functions that process signups.
  • Google Analytics — aggregate site-traffic analytics.

We do not share your information with any other third party for their own marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required to by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Flow, our users, or the public.

5. Flow Trial (the Chrome extension)

Flow Trial runs in your browser on Gmail. What it does with your mail is the part most worth being precise about, so this section describes it in full.

  • What it reads. When you open an email in Gmail, the extension reads that message's visible text, subject, and sender in order to decide whether it records a decision worth logging.
  • Where that decision is made. On your device, and only on your device. The code that scores a message performs no network requests of any kind — no email text is transmitted anywhere in order to reach that judgment. This is an architectural property of the extension, not a policy promise.
  • What leaves your device, and only when you ask. Nothing is written anywhere unless you click the Do It button on a specific message. When you do, Flow sends to the system you connected — Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, or Monday.com — a short summary label, the sender's name and email address, any amount or date it extracted, and a single quoted sentence from the message (up to 1,800 characters). That data goes directly from your browser to that provider. It does not pass through us and we do not receive a copy.
  • Connection credentials. Authorising a connector stores an access token in your browser's local extension storage on your own machine. We never receive or store these tokens. The one exception is the moment of authorisation itself: the provider's one-time authorisation code is exchanged for a token through our server, because that exchange requires an application secret that cannot safely be shipped inside an extension. We do not retain the code or the resulting token.
  • What stays local. Your chosen connector and line of work, the activity log of what Flow noticed and wrote, and the sensitivity calibration all live in extension storage on your device. None of it is sent to us. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.
  • What we do not do. We do not read your mailbox, do not receive your email content, do not sell any of it, and do not use any of it to train AI models — ours or anyone else's.

Flow Trial is intended for general business use. It is a separate product from Flow, it is not designed for regulated or otherwise sensitive material, and it carries none of the contractual or compliance guarantees described on our Security page.

6. Enterprise enquiries

If you submit the form on our Contact page, we collect what you enter there — your email address and, where you provide them, your name, organisation, role, approximate number of seats, preferred deployment model, timeline, and message. We use it to answer your enquiry and to keep a record of the conversation. It is stored with Supabase and notified to us by email through Resend.

7. Cookies

Google Analytics sets standard analytics cookies to distinguish returning visitors and measure site usage in aggregate. You can block or clear these at any time through your browser settings without affecting your ability to use the site or join the waitlist.

8. Data Retention

We keep waitlist submissions and enterprise enquiries for as long as the signup or conversation is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for our own records, unless you ask us to delete them sooner (see Section 10). Data held inside the Flow Trial extension lives on your own device and is removed when you uninstall it.

9. International Transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on those providers' own standard contractual safeguards for cross-border transfers.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent to future emails at any time. Our emails are transactional — a confirmation link, the Playbook you asked for, and install instructions — rather than a marketing newsletter, so they do not carry an unsubscribe footer; to stop hearing from us, or to exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@theflow-ai.com and we'll act on your request promptly.

11. Children's Privacy

Flow is a business-to-business product. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect information from, anyone under 16 years old.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do; material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@theflow-ai.com.
