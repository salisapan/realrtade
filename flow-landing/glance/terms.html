Terms of Service

Draft version 2026-10-15. Pending legal review.

These terms cover the Glance signup on this website and the personal product you install afterward. Flow, the organization product, is a separate agreement. Glance is a personal layer in your browser. It notices what you meant to do and closes it, with a receipt and Undo. It never sends without your approval. There is no password. The email you enter is the account.

The account

You must give an email address you control, and you must be old enough to agree to a contract where you live. We store that address with the version of these terms you accepted and the time you accepted them. That record is the account. We do not ask for a password.

Consent and versions

The signup button stays off until the email is valid and you check the terms box. Checking it records terms version 2026-10-15 and privacy version 2026-10-15. A later version replaces this page. The copy you accepted stays the copy on your row. The product-updates box is separate, starts unchecked, and is not required to get Glance.

Product updates

If you check the optional box, we send one confirmation email. You join the list only after you click the button in that email. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops the list and does not delete the terms record. You can also write to hello@theflow-ai.com.

Install

Chrome installs Glance only from the Chrome Web Store. After a successful signup we send you there. We do not offer a zip file. iPhone is not available. A later App Store release will have its own terms.

What you approve

Glance prepares a close and waits. A receipt names what was written. Undo removes what Glance just wrote, while it still can. A draft in a chat box is not a sent message. Glance never sends without your approval. You review anything financial or legal before you rely on it. Glance is for ordinary work, not for regulated or specially sensitive material, and it does not carry the commitments on the Security page. Those belong to Flow.

Deletion

You may ask us to delete the signup row. Email hello@theflow-ai.com. When the deletion endpoint is deployed, that request will go there as well. Uninstalling the browser product removes what it stored on the device.

Law

These terms are drafted for people in Israel and in the European Economic Area. The Israel Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, and the GDPR are described in the privacy draft of the same version. Mandatory consumer rights in your country still apply. If a court finds one sentence unenforceable, the rest remains.
