Running AI on your own infrastructure for regulated work

Regulated teams ask two questions: where the data goes, and who can compel access to it. Careful cloud processing can be enough when payloads are masked and minimized. When policy forbids raw material from leaving your control, the work has to stay on your machine or in a tenant you control. Flow can be scoped to either path. Flow-Edge, the fully local deployment, is an offer in rollout (בפריסה), not a runtime that is live for every customer today.

Residency is not sovereignty

Residency is where the bytes sit. Sovereignty is who can demand them. A region pin answers the first question. It does not, by itself, answer a compel, a subpoena, or a vendor that can read the payload. Name which question the policy is actually asking before you pick an architecture.

When cloud can be enough

A masked or minimized payload, a contract that limits use, and a human gate before any write can be enough for some teams. That is an evaluation. It is not a blanket yes, and it is not cover for pasting privileged material into any SaaS chat.

When your own infrastructure matters

Government, health, legal privilege, and finance policies often forbid raw egress. In those cases processing stays on the customer machine or in a controlled tenant. The policy decides. A slogan about "private AI" does not.

How Flow talks about this

Flow's architecture is local-first and scoped to the engagement, so sensitive work can stay on your side when that is the requirement. Masked cloud is the option where a minimized payload in a controlled tenant fits the policy. Flow-Edge is the deployment offer when policy requires processing on infrastructure you control. Common questions

Is cloud AI enough for HIPAA, GDPR, or government work?

Sometimes, with controls: masking, minimization, contracts, and a human gate before a write. Often the policy requires a tighter path. Evaluate the policy. This page does not claim a blanket yes, and it is not legal advice.

What is Flow-Edge status?

Flow-Edge is an offer / roadmap / in rollout (בפריסה). It is not a live shipped claim for every customer today. When policy requires processing to stay on your side, ask about that deployment option.

Does Flow send all documents to a public LLM by default?

No. The design is local-first, with human-gated writes. Masked cloud is an option where the policy allows a minimized payload. This page does not mean everything goes to a public model.
