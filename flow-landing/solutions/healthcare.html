AI automation for healthcare.

PHI does not get to leave the boundary because a vendor's roadmap says so. Flow deploys in a masked secure cloud — or entirely inside your own infrastructure — so automation is possible without renegotiating your HIPAA posture.

The constraint

Healthcare organizations are not short of automatable work: prior authorization packets, referral intake, claims correspondence, chart summarization for administrative review, cross-state licensure checks in telehealth. What they are short of is a way to automate any of it without routing protected health information through a system whose retention and subprocessor behavior cannot be fully accounted for in a BAA.

How Flow addresses it

  • Fully local deployment. With Flow-Edge, PHI never crosses your network boundary. This is the deployment model for organizations whose policy simply prohibits external processing.
  • Masking before processing. In the cloud model, identifiers are detected and masked before any data is processed.
  • Append-only audit trail. Every observation, proposal, approval, and execution is recorded — the evidence base for an OCR inquiry or an internal investigation.
  • Human approval on high-risk actions. Clinical and billing consequences do not get automated away silently.
