AI in healthcare: where PHI is processed

For a clinic or a health insurer, the first question about any AI tool is not what it can do. It is where protected health information goes while it does it.

Oct 28, 2026·3 min read

In short

  • Use and send only the minimum health information a task needs.
  • De-identification has defined methods; removing names alone is not enough.
  • Where processing runs is a design choice that should be made per process.

Minimum necessary

Under the HIPAA Privacy Rule, covered entities must make reasonable efforts to limit protected health information to the minimum necessary to accomplish the purpose of a use, disclosure or request (45 CFR 164.502(b)). For automation, that means a scheduling task does not need the diagnosis, and a billing task does not need the clinical notes.

De-identification

HIPAA sets two recognised methods for de-identifying health information in 45 CFR 164.514: removing a defined list of identifiers, or a documented determination by a qualified expert. Masking names in an email is helpful, but it is not, by itself, de-identification in the legal sense.

Choosing where it runs

  • In a cloud service with masking, identifying details are replaced before processing.
  • On employees' computers or an office server, the content does not travel to an external cloud.
  • In both, access, approvals and logs should be set before the first patient record is touched.
