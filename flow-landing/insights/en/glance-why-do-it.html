Why Do It, not auto-act

An assistant that acts by itself sounds faster. For anything that goes out in your name, one explicit click is faster in the end, because you never have to clean up after it.

Oct 28, 2026·3 min read

In short

  • Ready is not the same as done: Glance prepares, you close.
  • One click approves all the steps you can already see.
  • Pause and undo are always there.

Your name is on it

A reply to a client, a meeting change, a file shared with someone: each goes out as you. A wrong action is not a small error; it is something you have to explain. That is why Glance prepares everything and waits.

Why not automatic

  • Requests are often ambiguous. A person sees in a second what a system may miss.
  • Context lives outside the email: you know the client prefers mornings, or that the file is not final.
  • Mistakes multiply. A system that acts alone repeats a wrong assumption every time.

Why one click is enough

Glance shows what it understood and every step it will take. You read it once and click Do It. You do not approve each step separately, and you do not redo the work yourself. If something is off, you uncheck a step, add one, or pause.

After the click

The steps run in your tools, and you can see what was done. If you change your mind, cancel is right there.

The principle

Glance does not send on your behalf. It closes what you approve, in the tools you already use.
