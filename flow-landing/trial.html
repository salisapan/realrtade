FAQ

Questions worth asking first

Do I talk to it, or prompt it?

No. There’s no chat window and nothing to ask it. Glance stays on what is still open, and when something real needs to happen it shows exactly one button. You click, it writes the record, and it goes quiet again — nothing to prompt, manage, or hold a conversation with.

Is there AI in this?

Yes, as the engine and not the product. Reading what a message asks for, and what a reply really means, runs on your own device first, with our own rules and a small on-device model, and nothing about it needs a server. Only the optional Pro drafts and attachment summaries ever leave your machine, with names, amounts and dates masked first. What you see is simpler: what is still open, and when it is closed.

What does clicking Do It actually do?

It creates a real record in your own Google account — a Calendar event, a Task, or a drafted Gmail reply — with the amount, the date, the sender, one quoted sentence and a link back to the email. Every write comes with an Undo, and undoing it removes the record.

What does Glance do when I am waiting on someone?

When you send a message that asks for something — an answer, a signature, a payment — Glance offers one small card: remind me on the day to chase. If you say yes it adds a Google Task for that day, and when the person replies it completes the Task for you. When the reply is a question back (“which invoice?”) or “I never got the attachment”, it does not count as done: the loop stays open, the chasing stops, and the reminder becomes yours, with one button to prepare a first draft of your reply in Gmail (nothing is sent). A plain “no” closes it, as a no. An answer that arrives in a new thread about the same invoice settles the same loop. How does it decide when to speak?

It scores the open message for real signals — a currency figure, a commitment, a deadline, a stated loss — and stays quiet on things like price lists or automated mail. It speaks only above a confidence threshold, and that threshold adjusts as you click and dismiss. If you ignore a suggestion it disappears and Glance won’t ask about that message again. All of it runs on your device.

Is my email sent to a server?

Not to decide. That scoring happens on your machine. Glance makes three kinds of outbound call and no others: the writes you approve (Do It, or a follow-up reminder), straight to the Google account you connected; an anonymous usage ping, tagged to a random install ID and carrying no message content; and, only if you subscribe to Glance Pro and ask for a draft or an attachment summary, that text with names, companies, amounts and dates masked on your device first. The privacy policy lists every event in that last one.

Can I remove my data?

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored on your machine. To remove your email from our list, contact hello@theflow-ai.com.
