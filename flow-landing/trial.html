01

What it is — and isn't

It is

  • Silent by default — it only speaks up when something is actually decided. Most days it says nothing at all.
  • Zero-typing — no prompts, no chat box. You never write anything to it.
  • Cross-app — it writes the record into Notion or your CRM, not just Gmail.
  • Local — the reading happens on your device. Your email text isn't sent anywhere to make that call.
  • Reversible — every write comes with an Undo button.

It isn't

  • A chatbot. There's nothing to ask it and no conversation to have.
  • A rule builder. There's no "when X happens, do Y" screen to set up.
  • A notifier. It doesn't ping you to check in — going quiet for days is normal.
  • An inbox scanner. It only reads the one email you have open, nothing else.

04

What it does with your data

The short, precise version — not a generic privacy paragraph.

What it touches

  • The one email open in Gmail's reading pane — not your inbox, not older messages, not attachments
  • When you click Do It: the system you connected, to create one new record there
  • On your machine only: which system you connected, the kind of work you chose, and a log of what Flow suggested

What it never does

  • Never sends your email anywhere to decide. That judgment runs on your device.
  • No ad targeting, no reselling, no training a model on your mail
  • Never edits or deletes anything — it only adds one record, and gives you Undo
  • Never scans your mailbox in the background
05

Questions worth asking first

Does clicking Do It really change anything?

Yes. It creates a real record in the system you connected — a page in your Notion database, or a Note on the matching HubSpot Contact — with the amount, the date, the sender, the quoted sentence and a link back to the email. Every write comes with an Undo, and undoing it removes the record.

How does it decide when to speak?

It scores the open message for real signals — a currency figure, a commitment, a deadline, a stated loss — and stays quiet on things like price lists or automated mail. It speaks only above a confidence threshold, and that threshold adjusts as you click and dismiss. All of it runs on your device.

What do I have to configure?

Two things, once: where Flow may write, and what kind of work you do. No trigger screen, no field mapping, no rule syntax.

Is my email sent to a server?

Not to decide. That scoring happens on your machine. The only outbound call goes to the system you connected, and only when you click Do It.

What if my Notion database has different columns?

Flow adapts. It fills the title, then matches the rest by name and type — an Amount column, a Due date column, an email column, and so on. Anything it can't map still goes into the page body, so nothing gets dropped.

What happens if I ignore a suggestion?

It disappears and Flow won't ask about that message again. Both clicking and dismissing quietly tune how confident it needs to be before speaking up next time.

How do I install it?

Confirm your email, download the file, and load it into Chrome — under a minute. The install steps below walk through exactly what to click.

Can I remove my data?

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored on your machine. To remove your email from our list, contact hello@theflow-ai.com.
