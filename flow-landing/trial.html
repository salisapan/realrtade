A free, lean version of Flow. A browser extension that watches Gmail quietly, and — once in a while, when something actually changed — surfaces one Do It button. No chat window. No prompts to write. No if-this-then-that rules to configure.

What it is, and deliberately isn't

It is

  • Passive — silent until there's a real reason to speak
  • Zero-prompt — you never type anything, at setup or at runtime
  • Cross-platform — correlates what's in your inbox against a system outside Google's own ecosystem
  • Judgment-based — the model decides what's actionable, live, each time

It isn't

  • A chat assistant
  • A rule builder — there is no "when X happens, do Y" screen. That's Zapier, and it's exactly the configuration burden Flow exists to remove
  • A nagging notifier — if nothing happens for two days, that's the product working correctly

How it works

  1. Install & connect (~60 seconds)

    Install the extension, connect Gmail, connect one work system, answer one question about what kind of work you do. That's it — no field mapping, no rules.

  2. It stays quiet

    Most days, nothing happens. That's by design — a tool that talks when it has nothing to say gets uninstalled by day two.

  3. The moment

    An email arrives that actually changes something — a client confirms a price, a deadline moves. A small chip appears next to that message. Not a popup. Not a notification.

  4. Click, or ignore

    Click and it's done, with an undo link. Ignore it and it disappears and never asks about that email again. Both responses quietly retune the threshold.

What Flow Trial does with your data

Because this reads Gmail content, it deserves a precise answer rather than a generic privacy paragraph:

What it touches

  • The single message open in Gmail's reading pane — not your inbox, not older messages, not attachments
  • A compact snapshot of the relevant record in your connected work system, so the model can compare the two

What it never does

  • No ad targeting, no reselling of data, no use for training a model
  • No write happens without a click; there is no autonomous send, delete, or bulk edit
  • No crawling your mailbox in the background — only the message you have open

Message text is sent to a cloud endpoint for the moment it takes to judge it, then discarded — it is not written to any database. Your connector choice, domain choice, and activity log (shown/clicked/dismissed) live only in your browser's local storage; uninstalling the extension deletes them. Questions worth asking before you install

Does Flow Trial read all of my email?

It reads the message you have open in Gmail's reading pane, one at a time, only to decide whether it matches something in your connected work system. It does not crawl your inbox or read messages you haven't opened.

Is my email content stored anywhere?

Message text is sent to a cloud endpoint for a moment to judge whether an action is warranted, and is not retained afterward. The connector, domain choice, and activity log stay on your device in local browser storage.

Why isn't Flow Trial on the Chrome Web Store yet?

It's a pre-release developer preview. Submission requires a completed data-use disclosure and store review, which happens once the first connector is live.

What happens if I ignore a suggestion?

The chip disappears and Flow will not ask about that same email again. Both a click and a dismissal quietly retune how confident the system needs to be before it speaks up next time.

Can I remove my data?

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything stored locally. To remove your waitlist email, contact hello@theflow-ai.com.

Install it now (developer preview)

Already got the confirmation email, or just want to try it immediately? It's not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so installing it takes a few manual steps — same as any extension in active development.

  1. Download the ZIP file below and unzip it to a folder on your computer.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top-right toggle).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  5. Click the Flow icon in your toolbar, pick a connector and a work domain, and save.
