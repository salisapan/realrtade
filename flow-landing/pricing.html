Pricing

How Flow is priced.

Flow is in pre-release, and pricing is set per deployment. This page explains what actually drives the number, so you can size a budget before you talk to us.

What determines cost

Deployment Model

The masked secure cloud and a fully local Flow-Edge installation have materially different cost structures. Flow-Edge runs on your infrastructure, so you carry the compute and we license the engine.

Scope of Workflows

How many workflows are automated, and how deeply they reach into your systems — a single document pipeline is not the same as an engine acting across a department.

Seats

How many people have Flow observing and acting alongside their work.

Compliance Requirements

Environments requiring specific controls, evidence, or deployment isolation carry additional implementation work. We would rather scope that honestly up front than discover it at contract stage.

Why there is no price list yet

Publishing a per-seat number would be misleading for this product. A fully local deployment inside a hospital network and a masked cloud deployment for a twelve-person firm are different engagements, and the difference is not a discount tier. During pre-release we scope each deployment directly.

If you need a budgetary figure for a planning cycle, say so in your first email — we will give you a range with the assumptions written out, rather than asking you to sit through a call for it.

Early access

Flow is working with a limited number of design partners ahead of general availability. Early partners get direct input into the deployment model and roadmap. Join the waitlist to be considered.

Tell us your deployment model and rough scope, and we will come back with a range.
