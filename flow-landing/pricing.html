What can move the Enterprise number

The per-seat price above is the starting point. These are the things that change it.

Deployment Model

The masked secure cloud and a fully local Flow-Edge installation have materially different cost structures. Flow-Edge runs on your infrastructure, so you carry the compute and we license the engine.

Custom Connectors

Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack and Monday.com are included. A system we haven't built yet is scoped and priced separately, not bundled into the seat price.

Seats

How many people have Flow observing and acting alongside their work — volume discounts apply above 50 seats.

Compliance Requirements

Environments requiring specific controls, evidence, or deployment isolation carry additional implementation work. We would rather scope that honestly up front than discover it at contract stage.

Need a budgetary figure for a planning cycle? Say so in your first email — we will give you a range with the assumptions written out, rather than asking you to sit through a call for it.

Ready to talk Enterprise?

Tell us your deployment model and rough scope, and we will come back with a range.
